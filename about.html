Who we are

We are a team of six working on our Web Design and Development assignment. The brief started as a single-page portfolio. We turned it into a multipage website with real projects, real people and links that work.

Between us we have built privacy tools, a security checker, multiplayer and arcade games, and a logic circuit simulator. Everything on this site links to the real thing.
