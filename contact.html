Questions

How long does a project take?

A one-page site takes about 2 days. Standard projects usually take 4–7 days depending on how much is custom.

Do I see anything before I pay?

Yes. A free demo is built first so you can judge the design before committing.

Is hosting included?

Starter and Business plans include one month of free hosting on Cloudflare. After that, hosting is part of the ₹599/month maintenance plan.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can move from a smaller plan to a larger one whenever your business grows.
